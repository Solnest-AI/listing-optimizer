# StoryBrand SB7 — the emotional layer inside ALE copy

> SB7 is **not a separate framework**. It shapes *how* the ALE copy (title, summary, "The Space", captions) is written so the guest **feels they must book**. Every ALE copy output should pass this rubric.

The one rule: **the guest is the Hero, not the property.** The listing is the Guide that gets the Hero to their happy ending.

---

## The 7 elements (apply to every listing's copy)

1. **A Character (the Hero = the guest)**
   Lead with *who this is for* and *what they want*, not with the house. ("For families chasing first chairs and easy evenings…")

2. **Has a Problem** — three layers, name at least the external + internal:
   - **External:** needs a place to stay near the slopes/lake/event.
   - **Internal:** wants to feel relaxed, cared-for, like a good host/parent.
   - **Philosophical:** travel *should* be effortless — you shouldn't have to pack the whole house or stress the logistics.

3. **Meets a Guide (the host / management brand)**
   Show two things:
   - **Empathy:** "We get it — getting a crew up the mountain is a project."
   - **Authority/credibility:** superhost signals, 5★ review quotes, "thoughtfully stocked down to the dish pods", local partnerships.

4. **Who gives them a Plan** — make booking feel simple and de-risked. A clear 3-step: **Book → Arrive (easy self check-in) → Enjoy (everything's ready).**

5. **And calls them to Action**
   - **Direct CTA:** "Book your dates now."
   - **Transitional CTA:** "Message us — we'll help you pick the right room setup."

6. **That helps them avoid Failure**
   Paint the stressful stay they *won't* have: the cramped condo, the missing gear, the long drive to the lifts, the kids melting down. Lightly — one line, not fear-mongering.

7. **And ends in Success**
   Paint the trip they *will* have: boots on at first chair, hot-tub soak as the peaks go gold, arcade laughs after dinner, everyone sleeps well, you actually relax.

---

## How SB7 maps onto the ALE copy channels

| ALE copy channel | SB7 job |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Hero + the #1 desire/amenity in ~50 chars ("Family Ski Retreat \| Hot Tub • Arcade • Ski-in/out") |
| **500-char Summary** | Hero + Problem (internal) → Guide (empathy+authority) → Plan/Location → **direct CTA**. Mirrors ALE's 4 jobs. |
| **"The Space"** | Guide authority via specifics + Success painted room-by-room; keyword-loaded but still story-driven |
| **Captions** | micro-Success moments — sell the *feeling* of each shot, not the furniture |

## SB7 pass/fail check on any draft
- [ ] Is the **guest** the hero (not the house)?
- [ ] Is at least the **internal problem** named?
- [ ] Does the host show **empathy + authority**?
- [ ] Is there a **direct CTA** and a **transitional CTA**?
- [ ] Is **Success** painted (the trip they'll have)?
- [ ] Is **Failure** lightly avoided (the stay they won't have)?
- [ ] Voice still casual/warm — never corporate?
